# Credits

## The game

**1NSANE** (2000) was made by **Invictus Games** and published by **Codemasters**. 1NSANE:
Recompiled is an unofficial fan project, not made, endorsed or supported by them or by the
game's current owners. It contains none of the game's code or data: it works only with your
own copy of the game (the GOG release), translated on your own computer.

## This project

- **Anthony Airdo** ([AntAir267](https://github.com/AntAir267)): the project, its direction and
  testing; the made-up AI drivers' names.
- Written with **Claude** (Anthropic).

## Fan content

The fan cars and maps the game can load were made by the **1NSANE modding community** over
many years. They are not part of this repository; the fan pack has its own repository and
credits.
